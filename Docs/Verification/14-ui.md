## Snapshot, formatting and lifecycle policy

HUD attaches/detaches explicitly, refreshes immediately on initialization/re-enable/state visibility, and renders SnapshotChanged values. Display cache keys only avoid reformatting unchanged values; no resource/score model or render-frame string loop. Speed and percentage round to nearest nonnegative integer, halves upward (`floor(value+.5)`): actual22m/s → **79 km/h**, actual12.5 →45; Paused/GameOver →0. Fuel fill is Clamp01(Fuel/FuelCapacity); percent is the rounded normalized value. Capacity must be finite and positive and invalid setup is reported before division. LOW FUEL means the actual normalized value is <.25, independently of percentage rounding (24.5% displays25% and LOW FUEL). Lives use pale/lost silhouettes plus `n / 3`. Scores use invariant **integer** formatting, avoiding float precision loss in TMP's numeric overload.

GameManager commits state/timeScale/actual speed, applies the coordinated loop lifecycle, publishes the snapshot and StateChanged, then retains/emits the finalized RunEnded value/reason. Game Over fields consume RunEnded; re-enabling an existing GameOver panel reads GameManager.FinalRunSnapshot. BeginRun clears that result only after a valid reset. HUD may stay visible around terminal/pause panels and therefore shows frozen actual0 and exact zero terminal resources. Best remains session/persistence-owned and can increase during a run. Buttons never reset audio/VFX/player/pools independently and never expose Playing→Restart or GameOver→Menu.

The existing reset transaction, terminal priority, actual-speed audio/neon, ≤180m fuel guarantee and camera ownership remain intact. A concurrent user-authored mixer change was explicitly approved for retention: **RunnerMixer Master snapshot +4.552889 dB** (previously implicit0). No other audio change was made. Task13's listening/zero-clipped-sample capture predates this gain and is not asserted as a fresh review of it.
